# Boardz privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Boardz is an iPhone app for LED climbing boards. It's a personal open-source project, built on [Boardsesh](https://github.com/boardsesh/boardsesh). This page says what the app does with your data.

## The short version

Boardz has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The person who makes Boardz receives none of your data. What you sign in with and log goes to Boardsesh, the service Boardz is built on. Everything else stays on your phone.

## What goes to Boardsesh

Boardz uses Boardsesh's service at `ws.boardsesh.com` for accounts, climbs and logbooks. When you use it:

- **Signing in** sends your email and password to Boardsesh. The sign-in token Boardsesh sends back is kept in your iPhone's keychain.
- **Logging a climb** saves it to your Boardsesh logbook: the climb, the angle, your attempts, your rating and grade, and any note you add.
- **Searching climbs, rankings, beta videos and your profile** are read from Boardsesh.

Boardsesh's own [privacy policy](https://www.boardsesh.com/privacy) covers how it handles that data. Boardz isn't made or endorsed by Boardsesh.

To delete your account and everything in it, tap **Delete account** in Boardz's Settings. It opens your Boardsesh account settings, where deletion happens.

## What stays on your phone

Your lists (Favourites, Projects and your own), your board setup, your settings, and the sessions and workouts you run are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Bluetooth and the microphone

Boardz uses Bluetooth only to light holds on your board. It sends the board which holds to light, and nothing about you.

iOS lists a microphone permission because the sound library Boardz uses for workout beeps includes recording features. Boardz never records audio and never asks for the microphone.

## Links

Beta videos, password resets and boardsesh.com open in your browser or in the app that hosts them, such as Instagram or YouTube. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
